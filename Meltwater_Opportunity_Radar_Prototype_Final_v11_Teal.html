Competitive Intelligence

Adds the market-movement layer: competitor launches, positioning changes, campaign momentum, share-of-voice shifts and emerging weaknesses that reveal where a brand can differentiate or act first.

Competitor movesShare of voicePositioning gapsCampaign momentum
What GenAI adds

AI visibility opportunities

Surface prompts and answer spaces where a competitor is recommended more often, where the brand is absent, or where third-party source coverage can improve discoverability.

  • AI recommendation whitespace
  • Competitor answer dominance
  • Missing or weak cited-source coverage
  • Prompt clusters with commercial intent
What Competitive Intelligence adds

Competitive & whitespace opportunities

Track competitor momentum, positioning and market gaps, then connect those shifts to media, social, creator and AI evidence to identify where the brand can differentiate and respond.

  • Competitor campaign and launch momentum
  • Share-of-voice gains and losses
  • Positioning and message whitespace
  • Competitor weaknesses and response windows
Resulting engine

One opportunity layer

The combined engine can rank opportunities using a broader evidence base and keep learning from outcomes and human review before recommendations are activated.

  • Richer opportunity confidence
  • More precise brand-fit scoring
  • New AI + competitive opportunity types
  • Feedback-informed model improvement
